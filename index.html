About Bec

Hello, I'm Bec.

I have been a teacher of music for over 20 years, through classroom music teaching, instrumental teaching, conducting choirs and orchestras, and developing bands. I love finding what interests and drives a person musically, and then going with the flow, following their lead in how they wish to learn and develop their skills.

I have a BA (Hons) in Theatre Studies and a Postgraduate Certificate in Education, and I am currently conductor and arranger for the Aviva Choir, Norwich.

Corporate away days

A proper change of pace for your team.

A relaxed, music-led session for offsites and away days — the kind of thing people are still smiling about weeks later. No instruments or experience required, just a willingness to join in.

  • Format

    Half-day or full-day sessions, run at your offices or an external venue, and sized to suit your team.

  • Content

    Group singing, rhythm and simple ensemble work — light-hearted, low-pressure, and genuinely fun.

  • Outcome

    A shared, memorable experience that supports connection and wellbeing across the team.
